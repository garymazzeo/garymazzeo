This mini-site was created to promote *Parmigiano Reggiano*, and highlight the differences between the dairies from which the cheese is made in Italy. My favorite parts about it are the things I learned and tried out, like the use of SVGs for all of the illustrations, and using CSS's border-image for the "tasting notes" section on the [dairy pages](http://www.zingermanscommunity.com/parmproject/valserena/). I took a style that was previously made for a printed newsletter and faithfully reproduced it on the web.
