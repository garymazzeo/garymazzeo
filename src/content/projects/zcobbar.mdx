We affectionately call this the ZCoBbar (Zingerman's Community of Businesses navigation bar). It lives on every Zingerman's business footer, and houses all the legal links, along with a full listing of businesses in the 'CoB for easy traversing. I utilized local system fonts and SVGs to make the interface load quickly and look clean. Built with sustainability, accessibility, and fluid screen sizes in mind.
